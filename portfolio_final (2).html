Biggest Lesson

The Chatbot That Taught Me Everything

My first automation felt well designed.

It guided customers through renewal options, collected additional information, and passed everything to sales.

On paper, every step made sense. In practice, every extra question reduced the number of people who reached the end.

v1
BUSY Assistant
Bot v1.0

Your BUSY License is expiring on 30 June.
Click Renew Now to avoid interruption.

The automation worked perfectly.
The users didn't.

So I simplified the workflow.

One action. One response. One sales follow-up.
